## Citation key

`[<corpus-slug> § Heading › Subheading]` points at a heading in a corpus file; `[<corpus-slug> @ hh:mm:ss]` points at the bullet in a video note whose timestamp range covers that time. Video notes use the headings `Detailed source notes` (short notes) and `Research note` (article notes); skills use `Source text` and their own headings. Where a statement rests on a corrected transcript beyond the note text, the knowledge file says so. Because source notes are kept privately, resolve a citation by finding its slug in the source index below and following the "Original" link to the video or article; `@ hh:mm:ss` is the timestamp in that video, and `§ Heading` names the section of the article or skill. Skill citations resolve to the published files in `corpus/ai/`.

## Weighting

- **Skills (MIT full text)** are the most exact record of what each skill instructs, but they say nothing about when he formed the view, and the snapshot is from October 2026. They change quickly. The file's last-modified date is not a publication date.
- **Reviewed YouTube transcripts** are the best evidence of what he said. Of 335 caption tracks, 92 were read end to end by one designated reviewer and the remaining 243 by a Haiku review wave with Sonnet spot checks, so some notes are compressed and a missing topic is not evidence he never addressed it. The two caption-less Shorts have no intelligible speech and are noted from on-screen text.
- **Video notes** are reviewer paraphrase of auto-captioned speech with obvious mis-hearings corrected. Quote them as a report of what he said, not as his words.
- **Article notes** are around a paragraph each. Detail is thin for the tsconfig, `process.env`, npm package and `satisfies` articles in particular.
- **Guest and publisher videos** are used for his own answers. A guest's statements are attributed to the guest.
- **Dates:** a video's publication date is not necessarily when he formed the view. Several videos were first posted elsewhere as Shorts, and the AI Hero pages are undated.
- **Figures** such as token thresholds, parse speed-ups and compile times are his reported figures at recording time, not independent measurements.
