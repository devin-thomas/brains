# brains

Condensing the knowledge of great minds 🧠

A brain is a cited, structured digest of one expert's published work (principles, techniques, tradeoffs and per-topic knowledge) that an agent can consult. Every claim cites a source, and sources are indexed with their original URLs. A brain reports what the expert's published work says. It never speaks as the expert, claims their endorsement, or fills a gap with an opinion they have not published.
